## Who We Are

Shenzhen MindReset Technology Co., Ltd. (MindReset) is an innovative startup team in electronic products and interaction solutions, founded in March 2024 in Shenzhen, China. We have launched our core brand, Dot.

The Dot. brand is designed for young consumers who pursue a quality lifestyle, aiming to address the widespread issues of information overload and notification fatigue in modern society. Our ultimate goal is to enable tech products to blend gracefully into home or office environments, becoming personalized, non-intrusive “information windows” that help users stay focused and calm in the information age—bringing technology back to its original purpose of service in a more streamlined and efficient way.

Currently, the Dot. product lineup includes:

-   <Link href="/docs/quote_0"><strong>Quote/0</strong></Link>: A quiet e‑ink display that brings daily information, widgets, and automations into your space. Use the Dot. App to combine content and arrange how it appears, then tap with an NFC-capable phone to open more information or take action.
-   <Link href="/docs/rand_0"><strong>Rand/0</strong></Link>: A portable smart e‑ink device for everyday small questions and decisions. Through shake, dual-button, and NFC interactions, it offers lightweight tools such as the Book of Answers, dice, coin flips, random numbers, and quick decisions.
-   **More Innovations**: We are exploring the following products:
    -   **Read/0**: A 4.7-inch e‑ink reader development platform with a glass-and-aluminum body. It supports multi-touch, three touch buttons, TF card expansion, and user-flashable firmware—suitable for reading and for hands-on tinkering.
    -   **Clock/0**: A compact e‑ink clock with a 2.66-inch display and a coin-cell battery. It shows the time, temperature, and humidity, and is designed for extended use.
    -   **Quote/1**: The next generation of Quote/0, with a larger format, more capability, and interactive features.
    -   **Card/0**: A magnetic smart card.

We value efficient collaboration and continuous innovation, striving to fuse cutting‑edge technology with humanistic care to create products that truly meet user needs.
