---
title: How to Charge
---

Quote/0 charges with a Type-C cable and is compatible with most chargers.

A full charge takes about 1.5 hours. We recommend fully charging the device before first use.

After you connect the power, the status light next to the power port shows the charging state.

| Status light | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Orange or blinking | Charging |
| Steady white | Fully charged |
