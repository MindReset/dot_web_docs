This repository gives Rand/0 two useful modes. Check gold, silver, or two selected stocks with their price, percentage change, and update time. Or turn a TXT file into a paged reading screen and move through it with the device buttons.

### Put market data on the desk

- **Gold and silver**: Show Au99.99 gold and Ag T+D silver with the current price and percentage change. Custom titles and a background image are supported.
- **Selected stocks**: Enter two stock codes and names, then switch between them on the device. Each screen shows the current price, percentage change, and update time.
- **Choose a refresh rhythm**: The precious-metal page supports 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 3 minutes. The stock page supports 30 seconds or 1 minute.

### Turn TXT files into a page-by-page reader

- **Load and read**: Detect UTF-8 or GBK automatically and paginate the text for the 200 × 200 screen.
- **Tune the typography**: Adjust font size, font family, and bold text. V15 also lets you load a custom font.
- **Pick up where you left off**: Jump to a page or use the device buttons to turn pages. V15 remembers each book's reading position and keeps the reader settings locally.
