## Clock

Clock displays date, time, and weekday on Rand/0.

-   Shows date, time, and weekday.
-   If Clock needs a network connection, the device will guide you through Wi-Fi connection after a short prompt.
-   The first time you use Clock, Rand/0 may show an energy-use notice. Press either button to continue.

## Controls

1. Long press the bottom button to enter.
2. View the current date, time, and weekday.
3. If the device asks you to connect to Wi-Fi, follow the prompt. Long press the top button to cancel and return to the menu.
4. Long press the top button to return to the home screen.

Clock stays visible and refreshes the time as it changes. It uses more idle power than the normal lock screen, but it remains available without the usual sleep timeout.
