# Support

## Documentation feedback

For incorrect instructions, broken links, translation issues, or missing explanations, [open a documentation issue](https://github.com/MindReset/dot_web_docs/issues/new/choose). Include the page URL, language, and the passage or step involved. Use [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) if you would like to propose a fix.

## Product and account help

For device, account, purchase, or app support, use the channels listed on the [official contact page](https://dot.mindreset.tech/docs/contact). Do not post order details, account information, device identifiers, or personal content in public issues.

For Dot Skill installation, helper script, or MCP problems, use [Dot Skill support](https://github.com/MindReset/dot_skill/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).

## Security

Do not disclose vulnerabilities or exposed credentials in public issues. Follow [Security](SECURITY.md) for private reporting.
