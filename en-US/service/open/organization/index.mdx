An organization gives your team a shared place to manage members, device software, and publishing credentials.

## Quick start

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create an organization

Open **My account → My organizations** on the website and [apply to create an organization](/docs/service/open/organization/membership). Read and agree to the [Organization and Publisher Agreement](/docs/organization_terms) before submitting. After approval, open the organization from the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step>
### Invite members

An owner or administrator can [invite members by email, phone number, or join link](/docs/service/open/organization/membership). Invitees join when they accept.

</Step>
<Step>
### Start collaborating

Team members can manage [device software and versions](/docs/service/open/organization_software) together on the website. To call the API from CI or a release script, [set up organization credentials](/docs/service/open/organization/credentials).

</Step>
</Steps>
