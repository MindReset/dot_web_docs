## Timer

Timer gives you a simple way to give a task a beginning, an ending, or a little more time without reaching for your phone.

## Start a Timer

1. Open **Timer** and long press the bottom button.
2. On the first use, choose **Count up** or **Countdown**.
3. For a countdown, choose a duration from **5 to 95 minutes** in **1-minute steps**, then long press the bottom button to start.
4. Count up starts immediately from `00:00` and runs until it reaches `95:00`.

After you have saved a setup, choose **Repeat last** to use the previous mode and duration, or choose **Set new time** to configure it again.

## While the Timer Is Running

- Short press the top or bottom button to view a static time snapshot. Short press either button again to return to the status view.
- While running, long press the bottom button to pause.
- While paused, long press the bottom button to open **Resume** or **Exit**.
- Long press the top button at any time to leave Timer and return to the More folder.

## Sounds and Locking

When sound is enabled, a running Timer plays alternating tick and tock sounds. A countdown reaching zero or a count-up reaching its limit plays a completion alert. The first button press during the alert silences it.

A running or paused timer is not stopped by the normal sleep timeout. Leaving Timer, restarting the device, or losing power ends the timer; it will not resume automatically.
