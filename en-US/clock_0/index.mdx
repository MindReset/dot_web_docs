## What is Clock?

<CustomCloudImage
  osSrc="dot/docs/clock_0_1791859419646.png"
  alt="Clock e-paper clock"
  className="h-auto w-full max-w-100"
/>

Clock is a lightweight e-paper clock. The content on its 2.66-inch black-and-white display changes automatically at sunrise and sunset.

An included CR2032 battery powers the clock. The SET and BACK buttons on the rear let you set the date and time and refresh the screen. The magnetic rear cover lets you attach it to surfaces that attract magnets.
