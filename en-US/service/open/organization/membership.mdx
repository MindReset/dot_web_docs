An organization gives your team a shared place to manage members, roles, and resources. These steps use `Field Lab` as the example.

## Apply to create an organization

1. Sign in, open [My account → My organizations](/panel?tab=my-account&accountTab=organizations), and select **Apply to create an organization**.
2. Enter `Field Lab` as the organization name, add an optional avatar and description, and submit the application.
3. Check the status and any review feedback under **My applications**. Select **Edit and apply again** to revise an unsuccessful application.
4. After approval, open `Field Lab` from **My organizations** in the sidebar.

To share your organization ID with support, choose **Copy organization ID** from the actions menu at the top of the organization page.

## Invite members

Choose an invitation method under **Organization → Invitations**. Invitees join when they accept.

| Method | How the invitee joins |
| --- | --- |
| Email | Open the link in the invitation email, sign in with the corresponding verified email address, and accept. |
| Phone number | Sign in to the Dot. account with that verified phone number. Accept the invitation on the website under **My account → My organizations → Received invitations**. |
| Join link | Open the shared link, sign in to a Dot. account, and confirm that you want to join. |

Invitations and join links expire after 48 hours. You can review and cancel pending invitations or revoke a join link from **Invitations**.

You can share a join link with several people. An organization can have up to 100 members, including the owner. A place must be available when an invitee accepts or joins through a link.

## Manage roles and transfer ownership

| Role | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Member | View members, manage device software projects and versions on the website, and leave the organization. |
| Admin | Do everything a member can do, edit organization details, manage invitations and credentials, and remove members with the member role. |
| Owner | Do everything an admin can do, assign or remove admin roles, remove other members, and transfer ownership. |

From the organization’s actions menu, select **Transfer ownership**. Choose an existing member and confirm. The selected member becomes the owner and the former owner becomes a member. The former owner can leave once the transfer is complete.

The owner can also delete the organization from this menu. Deletion closes its API and public software downloads.

If Dot. suspends an organization, its page shows the suspension. Management, publishing, and public downloads pause. See the [Organization and Publisher Agreement](/docs/organization_terms) for how to appeal.
