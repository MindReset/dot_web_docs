title: Content Modes
description: Learn how Quote/0 uses loop and fixed content to organize what appears on the display.
---

Quote/0 offers two content modes for different display routines.

Loop content is suited to continuous rotation, while fixed content lets you show selected content during defined time windows. Choose a mode below to learn how it works and how to configure it.

<Cards>
	<Card href="/docs/quote_0/start/content_mode/loop" title="Loop Content">
		Keep content rotating in list order and adjust the switching interval.
	</Card>
	<Card href="/docs/quote_0/start/content_mode/fixed" title="Fixed Content">
		Show selected content on specific days and during defined time windows.
	</Card>
</Cards>
