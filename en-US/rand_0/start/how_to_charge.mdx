---
title: How to Charge
description: Learn how to charge Rand/0 and read its status light.
---

Rand/0 charges with a Type-C cable and is compatible with most chargers.

A full charge takes about 1 hour. We recommend fully charging the device before first use.

After you connect power, the status light next to the power port shows the charging state.

| Status light | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Orange-red | Charging |
| Steady white | Fully charged |
