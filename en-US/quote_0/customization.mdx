This page explains what can be customized for Quote/0, along with the applicable minimum order quantities, deposit requirements, and customer responsibilities. All deposit percentages are calculated from the total order amount.

## Hardware

| Customization item | Availability | Rules and limits | Minimum order | Deposit | Customer responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enclosure design | Conditional | Design an enclosure using the enclosure data provided by Dot. Dot. does not manufacture or assemble enclosures. See the [developer page](/developers) for development resources. | None | — | Complete the design, manufacturing, and assembly |
| Directly supplied components | Conditional | Directly supply components such as the display, mainboard, or battery. Dot. does not manufacture or assemble enclosures. | 2,000 | At least 40% of the total order amount | Complete design adaptation and assembly |
| Disassemble a finished device and assemble it | Available | Disassemble finished devices and complete the enclosure adaptation and assembly. | None | — | Complete the disassembly, design adaptation, and assembly |

## Device software

| Customization item | Availability | Rules and limits | Minimum order | Deposit | Customer responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Device software | Available | Device software can be customized. See the [developer page](/developers) for development resources. | None | — | Flash the device software yourself |

## Service content

| Customization item | Availability | Rules and limits | Minimum order | Deposit | Customer responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activation and network reconfiguration | Available | Customize the content shown when a user activates the device or reconfigures its network. Both actions use the same content. | None | — | Confirm the content to display |
| Default content after setup | Available | Show customized content after setup. If no customization is requested, the device displays “提示添加内容”. | None | — | Confirm the default content |
| Content Studio platform content | Conditional | Customize Content Studio platform content, provided it follows the requirements in [Join Content Studio](/docs/service/studio/join-content-studio). | None | — | Prepare the content materials and submit an application |

## Packaging

| Customization item | Availability | Rules and limits | Minimum order | Deposit | Customer responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Packaging design | Conditional | Packaging can be customized to the needs of the project. | 2,000 | At least 30% of the total order amount | Provide the packaging design |
