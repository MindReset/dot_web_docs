## Reset Device

1. Open **SETUP** on Rand/0.
2. Select **Factory Reset**.
3. Read the confirmation prompt and long press the bottom button to continue.

Factory Reset clears user settings, the home-screen layout, remembered Wi-Fi networks, and the custom wallpaper, then restarts Rand/0. This action cannot be undone.
