## Article 1. Parties and scope

This Agreement is between Shenzhen MindReset Technology Co., Ltd. (“we” or “us”) and the individual, legal entity, or unincorporated organization (“you”) that applies for or manages an organization on Dot. or submits, publishes, or distributes device software through an organization. A publisher is a party that submits, publishes, or distributes software on its own behalf or on behalf of a person or entity it represents.

This Agreement supplements the [User Agreement](/docs/terms). The appendix is part of this Agreement and has the same effect as the main terms. This Agreement governs organization management and device software publishing. The User Agreement governs other matters.

## Article 2. Acceptance and organization management

Before applying to create an organization, you must read and expressly accept this Agreement. It takes effect when you expressly accept it. If you accept it on behalf of another person or entity, you must have their authorization. If we create an organization on your behalf, its owner must accept this Agreement before using the software publishing service.

You must provide accurate and truthful application information and update it promptly when it changes. Applications to create organizations are subject to our approval.

Anyone responsible for managing an organization must manage member access and organization credentials in line with their role. When ownership changes, a member leaves, or permissions change, promptly review the relevant access and credentials and revoke them as appropriate. If you discover compromised credentials or unauthorized publishing activity, take prompt action and notify us.

## Article 3. Scope of services

We do not charge any fees under this Agreement for organization applications or management, device software uploads or hosting, downloads, or over-the-air (OTA) distribution.

## Article 4. Safety measures, suspension, and appeals

If you discover that a published version may put devices, data, or users at risk, you must immediately disable the affected version and take the follow-up steps in the appendix.

**We may immediately disable an affected version if you fail to disable it promptly or if urgent action is needed. For serious safety risks or violations, we may suspend the entire organization.** If software may be unlawful or infringing, or violates this Agreement, we may disable the affected version based on the circumstances and impact. We also comply with lawful requirements from competent authorities.

To the extent permitted by law, we will promptly notify the affected parties of the reason for suspension, its scope, and how to appeal.

To appeal a suspension, email [hi@support.mindreset.tech](mailto:hi@support.mindreset.tech). Include the organization's name or ID, the software and version if applicable, your reason for appealing, and supporting materials. We will review the appeal, decide whether to lift the restrictions, and let you know the outcome.

## Article 5. Infringement notices and counter-notices

Rights holders may email infringement notices to [hi@support.mindreset.tech](mailto:hi@support.mindreset.tech). Include the rights holder’s true identity and contact details, information identifying the software such as its name and link, and preliminary evidence of the rights held and the alleged infringement. When we receive a notice that meets the legal requirements, we promptly forward it to the relevant publisher and take necessary measures under applicable law.

If a publisher believes that its software does not infringe, it may submit a counter-notice to the same address. The counter-notice must include the publisher’s identity and contact details, information identifying the software, and preliminary evidence supporting its position. Under the Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China, the Regulations on Protection of the Right of Communication Through Information Networks, and other applicable law, we forward the counter-notice to the rights holder and explain that they may file a complaint with the relevant authority or bring a claim before a people’s court. We maintain or lift the measures in accordance with applicable law.

Anyone who submits a notice or counter-notice is responsible for the truthfulness of the information and materials provided. We handle personal information involved in complaints and appeals under applicable law and our [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy).

## Article 6. Liability

You are legally responsible for the materials and software you submit and for how you publish, distribute, and promote the software. We fulfill the obligations imposed on us as the platform operator. Each party is liable as provided by law for harm caused by its breach of this Agreement or infringement of others’ rights.

## Article 7. Changes and termination

We will publish updates to this Agreement and state when they apply. If a change materially affects you, including changes to the distribution license, suspension rules, or liability, we will prominently draw your attention to it and ask you to accept it again before it applies.

You may use the available platform options to withdraw software you have published or stop using the relevant services. Either party may terminate this Agreement in accordance with applicable law. When the relevant services end, we will stop distributing the software through them and handle record retention as described in the appendix. Rights and obligations that arose before termination, and disputes that remain to be resolved after termination, are governed by this Agreement and applicable law.

## Article 8. Governing law and dispute resolution

The laws of mainland China govern the formation, validity, performance, and interpretation of this Agreement, and disputes arising from it. Any applicable mandatory rules on governing law or jurisdiction prevail. The parties may first try to resolve disputes through discussion. If they cannot reach a resolution, either party may bring a claim before a people’s court with jurisdiction under applicable law.

If a provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions that can apply independently continue to apply as permitted by law. For questions about this Agreement or to request an explanation of the terms, [contact us](/docs/contact).

## Appendix. Device software publishing rules

### 1. Eligible devices

This service is available only for MindReset device models that the platform supports for software publishing. You must not use this service to upload, host, publish, or distribute software for devices from other brands.

If we find a violation of this section, we may refuse to publish or disable the affected version. For serious or repeated violations, we may suspend the entire organization. Article 4 governs suspensions and appeals.

### 2. Software rights and compliance

Before submitting software, make sure you have the rights to publish and distribute it and any third-party code, components, or assets it contains, and that you may grant the license in this appendix. This is subject to applicable law, including the Copyright Law of the People’s Republic of China and the Regulations on Computer Software Protection. Comply with applicable open-source and other license terms. Preserve rights notices, provide license texts, and make relevant source code available when required.

You must not submit or publish software that violates applicable law, infringes the rights of others, or contains malicious code. You must not use software to interfere unlawfully with devices or networks or to access or alter data unlawfully. If the software processes personal information, comply with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Law of the People’s Republic of China. Notify users as required, ensure that you have a lawful basis for processing, and obtain consent when required by law.

Intellectual property rights in the software and its contents remain with their respective rights holders. **To the extent you are authorized to do so, you grant us a non-exclusive license limited to providing the services under this Agreement.** We may store, copy, and review software and release materials and offer downloads and OTA updates to the users of your chosen release channel. You authorize us to use hosting and distribution providers to handle the materials as needed to provide these services.

The license applies in the regions where we provide services for the selected release channel, from software submission until the relevant software services end. If you withdraw software or the relevant services end, we will stop distributing it through this service. We handle materials subject to legal retention requirements under applicable law. We retain materials needed for complaints, appeals, or disputes only as long as necessary.

### 4. Publishing and ongoing safety

Provide accurate version, release channel, and device compatibility information. Tell users about known significant risks and any necessary installation or recovery requirements. Stable releases can be published only after we approve them under the platform’s publishing rules. Publishers are legally responsible for their software’s compatibility, safety, and maintenance.

If you discover a safety risk in published software, disable the affected version as required by Article 4. Promptly notify users who may be affected, report serious risks to us, and provide instructions for fixing the issue or recovering the device where possible.

**On software release pages, your own website, promotional pages, social media, and any other public channel, clearly identify the software as third-party software and give the name of the individual or organization that publishes it.**

You must not falsely claim to act in the official name of our company or any of our brands, products, or product lines. You must not use names, trademarks, logos, wording, page designs, or similar means to mislead the public into believing that we develop, publish, operate, or provide the software, that we officially certify, authorize, or endorse it, or that the publisher is officially affiliated with or partnered with us.
