This page explains what can be customized for Rand/0, along with the applicable minimum order quantities and deposit requirements. All deposit percentages are calculated from the total order amount.

## Hardware

| Customization item | Availability | Rules and limits | Minimum order | Deposit | Customer responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Housing color | Conditional | The frame and back cover use the same color. | 2,000 | At least 20% of the total order amount | Confirm the color scheme |
| Back-cover card | Conditional | The card size is 30 mm × 37 mm. | 2,000 | At least 20% of the total order amount | Prepare the card design |

## Device software
